## 5. Research Model, Simulated Results and Dry Runs

The strategy selector is a research model of a fruit fly's mushroom body. It chooses among hand-written strategies; it has not been shown to produce an edge, and most strategies tried did not work. Backtests, paper trading and dry runs use simplified fill, fee and slippage models and are not a guide to live outcomes. In this version the learning signal is computed from a simulated episode even when live orders are enabled, so live fills and the signal the model learns from can differ.
